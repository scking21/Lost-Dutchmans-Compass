Your discovery signal shows the resolved favorite, category and destination used for the completed request. Each card represents an actual Qloo-returned place, preserving its provider position and affinity when supplied. Shortlisting changes the suggested next step locally; it does not change Qloo’s scores. Open Place evidence for the entity ID and identity evidence, or Inspect source and request evidence for the safe request trace. These affinities describe aggregate patterns, not your identity or future behavior. The explanation on each card is Compass’s summary of the query, not a causal explanation supplied by Qloo. No historical narrative, itinerary, access permission or safe route is generated here. Check the venue's own information before visiting.

Built for this web app

This app runs the official @qloo/qloo-harness 0.1.26 on its server. Its describe, recommend and rank workflows use the official Qloo kit’s CLI route. Compass adds the browser interface, query-context summary, local shortlist and next-step guidance, plus expandable evidence. Edit your brief returns you to the favorite field; changing inputs clears stale results before another request.

What works, and what remains unverified?
